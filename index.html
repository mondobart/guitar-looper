To auto-calibrate, unplug your headphones and turn the volume up. You'll hear six clicks, and the looper times how long they take to come back through the mic. If your loops still sound late compared with the drums, nudge the slider up. If they sound early, nudge it down.
Keys / pedal: Space, Enter, → or Page Down = Record/Overdub · P = Play/Stop · ←, Page Up or Backspace = Undo. Most Bluetooth page-turner pedals work as a foot switch.
Mic: point it at the guitar near where the neck meets the body, about 30 cm away. Keep the level meter below the red.
